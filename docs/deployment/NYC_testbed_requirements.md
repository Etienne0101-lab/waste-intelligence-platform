# NYC Smart City Testbed Requirements Mapping

This platform aligns with pilot and smart-city objectives for operational efficiency, environmental monitoring, and public engagement.

## Alignment areas

- Real-world deployment readiness
- Measurable environmental impact
- Operational optimization
- Smart-city interoperability
- Security and compliance
- Transparency and auditable reporting

## Evaluation criteria

The platform supports multi-scale waste intelligence across source, cluster, facility, and borough perspectives, enabling objective performance tracking and operational decision-making.
